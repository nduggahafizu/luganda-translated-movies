Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you pay.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. Payments go through Pesapal, which takes MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Visa and Mastercard.

How do I subscribe?

Sign in, choose a plan above and pay in the Pesapal window that opens. Your plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed.

What do I get with a plan?

Every movie and series translated into Luganda, for as long as your plan lasts, and you can download on every plan. 2 Weeks and Monthly have no ads. Daily works on 1 device at a time; every other plan has no device limit.

Can I download movies?

Yes, on every plan, Daily included.

How many devices can I use?

On the Daily plan, 1 device at a time (a phone, a computer or a TV), with as many tabs as you like on it. Weekly, 2 Weeks and Monthly have no device limit.

Does my plan renew by itself?

No. Each plan is a single payment. When its days are up, choose another plan to keep watching.

Can I change my plan at any time?

Yes. A new plan starts straight away and replaces the one you have, so any days left on your old plan are not added on. It's best to switch when your current plan ends.

I paid but I still can't watch. What should I do?

Give Pesapal a minute to confirm the payment, then refresh the page. If it still doesn't work, message us on WhatsApp (+256 743 311 809) or email nduggahafizu@gmail.com with your payment reference.
