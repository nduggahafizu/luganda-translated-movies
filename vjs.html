About Our VJ Translators

A VJ (Video Jockey) is a Ugandan commentator who narrates over the original audio of a movie or series, translating dialogue into Luganda while adding context, jokes, and cultural commentary in real time. This style of translation grew out of Kampala's video halls, where a live VJ would narrate films for the audience, and it has since become one of Uganda's most popular ways to watch foreign movies and shows.
